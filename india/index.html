Our story

Rooted in Nashik, built for the long run.

Savora was founded by Geeta Sehgal at the start of 2026, in the heart of Nashik.

It began with a clear conviction: the snacks families reach for every day should be made with ingredients they recognise, and should support their health rather than work against it. Savora was created to prove that healthy snacking in India can be both responsible and genuinely enjoyable.

Our aim is to help build a healthier India through thoughtfully made snack ranges. Every Savora product is air fried instead of deep fried, and made without preservatives and without palm oil. We hold ourselves to that standard for today's generation and for the generations that come after.

Our promise

What goes into every pack

Three commitments we make on every product, without exception.

i

Nothing preserved

We do not use artificial preservatives. Our snacks are made fresh in small batches and sealed with care to keep them crisp.

ii

No palm oil

Palm oil never enters our kitchen. We keep our ingredients simple, recognisable and honest.

iii

Air fried, not deep fried

Air frying gives the crunch you expect with far less oil, so every handful feels lighter and sits better.
